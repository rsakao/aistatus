PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy

AI Status does not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal information to the developer. It contains no analytics, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, accounts, or telemetry.

Network requests

To display service health, the app connects directly over HTTPS to public status endpoints operated by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Cloud, Cursor, Perplexity, and GitHub. Those operators may receive standard connection metadata such as your IP address, request time, and User-Agent. Their respective privacy policies govern that data.

Local preferences

Your selected services, refresh interval, menu bar display, language, and launch-at-login state are stored on your Mac. They are not sent to the developer.

External links

Official status pages and GitHub links open in your default browser. From that point, the destination website and browser govern data handling.

This website

This GitHub Pages site adds no custom analytics, advertising, or cookies. GitHub's operation of Pages is governed by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in GitHub Issues. Do not include personal or sensitive information in a public issue.
